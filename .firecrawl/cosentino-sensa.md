### Sensa – FAQs

### [How to mix and match Sensa?](https://www.cosentino.com/sensa/\#1595423965696-251c5ea4-840cea2b-182c)

The stain-resistant protection of Sensa natural stone makes it easy to use for kitchen countertops and wall cladding. This makes it possible to create spaces with different applications to match and play off each other.

### [Where to buy Sensa?](https://www.cosentino.com/sensa/\#1601547316008-09eb2823-356c)

Find your local kitchen shops and Sensa suppliers with our store locator.

### [What formats and thicknesses is Sensa available in?](https://www.cosentino.com/sensa/\#1601547315127-1a4e0390-c352)

Sensa is available in 20 and 30 mm thicknesses for countertops and 30 mm for paneling.

### [Sensa maintenance](https://www.cosentino.com/sensa/\#1604505715879-2b94f1ee-9744)

Sensa cleaning and maintenance is very easy – just wash with soap and water. No further treatment is required to protect the stone.
