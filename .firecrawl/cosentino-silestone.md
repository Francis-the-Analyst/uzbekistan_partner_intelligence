## 2020 MARKS THE BEGINNING OF A NEW REVOLUTIONARY TECHNOLOGY THAT REDUCES THE CRYSTALLINE SILICA CONTENT IN SILESTONE

In 2020 we launched our revolutionary Hybriq+® technology, which drastically reduced the crystalline silica content in Silestone composition, blending premium minerals with recycled materials to create a new product category: Mineral Low-Silica Surfaces.

### SILESTONE AND THE Hybriq+® TECHNOLOGY

## SINCE 2023, ALL SILESTONE COLORS ARE MANUFACTURED WITH LOW CRYSTALLINE SILICA CONTENT POWERED BY Hybriq+®

### Hybriq+® IS THE RESULT OF YEARS OF INNOVATION AND INVESTMENT

In 2017, Cosentino anticipated the future by initiating a comprehensive research process to create the first low-silica mineral surfaces on the market. In 2020, thanks to Hybriq+® technology, we launched the first two low-silica collections: Loft and Sunlit Days. By 2023, we manufacture 85% of surfaces with less than 40% crystalline silica and 15% with less than 10%.
